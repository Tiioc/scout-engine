## Candidates
- C4  US 90, small value 10 (cleared core)
- C8  US 80, small value 20

(C1 US 100 shown for reference.)

## Tests
T1, T2 (both halves), T3 (1990-2026), T4 (Developed ex US, Europe, Japan), T6 crises, T7 bootstrap 1927-2026, T8 today's conditions (US lowered to ~6% nominal, bills 4%). Plus T8s: T8 with the small-value premium cut to half and to zero (the honest worry, since US small value trailed 2011-2026).

## Decision rule (fixed)
C8 or C9 replaces C4 only if ALL hold, judged against C4 (not C1):
1. T8 median ending wealth ahead of C4 by at least 3%.
2. Ahead of C4 in both T2 halves and in T3.
3. Same direction outside the US (T4) in at least 2 of 3 regions.
4. Deepest fall no more than 10 points worse than C4 in every test.
5. Chance of ending behind C4 in T8 below 50%.
6. With the premium cut to zero (T8s), it trails C4 by no more than 5% (a dead premium must not cost much).
7. Passes the safety gate.
If both pass, the higher T8 median wins unless within 3% of the other, then the smaller dose wins. If neither passes, C4 stays.
