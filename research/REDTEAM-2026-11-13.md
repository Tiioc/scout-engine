# Red Team on the growth mix, 2026-11-13

Verdict from the Red Team: KILL as written. The Chair re-ran the central claim independently and it holds.

## What was checked by the Chair (scripts/realfunds.py logic, same data, month-end rule)

| Start | Growth mix per year | SPY per year |
|---|---|---|
| 2008-01 | 11.1% | 11.0% |
| 2009-04 | 12.5% | 15.6% |
| 2010-01 | 11.2% | 13.9% |
| 2015-01 | 10.4% | 13.4% |
| 2020-01 | 11.1% | 14.7% |

- Equal monthly deposits from 2008-01: the mix ended 26% behind SPY.
- The mix beat SPY in 8% of 5-year windows and 9% of 10-year windows.
- The earlier "11.6% against 11.2% since 2008" depended on starting just before the 2008 crash.

## Red Team findings not yet re-run by the Chair (treat as unverified until re-run)

- Weekly rule checks every 5th trading day, not week-ends; across the five phases the deepest fall is 46% to 57%, and 49% to 55% with a one-day delay.
- frequency.py annualises over 104.1 years where the span is 99.9.
- Gold is yearly data smoothed to daily, so worst-day figures are understated.
- The 8.8% real growth figure is the best of 92,378 mixes at the loss limit; discount 2 to 3 points.
- The weekly 200-day rule has never been tested on real funds with daily data.
- `scout.py check` run on a Friday drops the week in progress.
- R8 and config disagree on weekly against monthly; R3 and config disagree on rebalance timing.
- M1 charges $3 a month under $10,000, which conflicts with hard rule 9.

## Consequence

The growth mix should not be presented as beating a total-market fund. What the exits buy is smaller falls, which matters when the balance is large, not at the start. A rule change for the growth stage needs the owner's approval (hard rule 10).
