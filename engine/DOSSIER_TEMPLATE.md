# Dossier template

One file per deep play, at `editions/dossiers/<play-id>.md`. The owner should be able to act from this file alone. Plain language. Every figure has a source and a date.

```
# <Play name>   (<play id>, <date>)
Kind: money play / build play        Confidence: A / B (one-line reason)

## The opportunity in three sentences
What it is, why it exists now, and why it is not already priced in or crowded.

## Verdict
Do this / do this only if <condition> / wait for <dated event>.
Bet: <share of portfolio and dollars, or hours per week and total hours>, from scripts/sizing.py.

## The numbers
Scenario table (each outcome, its probability, ending multiple or dollars).
Expected return and how it compares with simply holding the market.
Chance of losing money, average loss when it loses, worst case.
What the best and worst case would do to the whole portfolio.

## Evidence
What is confirmed (source, date). What is not confirmed. What comes from sellers.
The base rate for things like this, and why this case is better or worse.

## The strongest case against
Red Team's best argument, unedited.

## Entry: exactly what to do
1. The instrument or first step, and where to do it (account type, platform, office, website).
2. The price or condition to enter at, and what to do if it is not met.
3. Whether to enter all at once or in stages, with the amounts and dates.
4. Costs, minimums, taxes and paperwork to expect.
For a build play: the first three concrete tasks, the hours each takes, and the cheapest test that proves demand before real time or money goes in.

## Exit rules (set now, before any money or time goes in)
Take-profit or trim conditions. The condition that proves the thesis wrong. The stop: the loss or the hours at which this ends regardless. The review date.

## What the Scout will track
The dated events, data releases and filings it will watch, the forecasts logged, and what each result would mean.

## What the owner has to do each week
Usually nothing. If something is needed, the task and the time it takes.
```
